My Universe built around you.

Some stars enter quietly. And somehow,makes the whole world bright without any return. As if your path crossed mine.

Turn up your sound, take a slow breath… and scroll. ✦

Six months, one unexpected universe

We met through our besties, two wild souls, untamed and unrestrained.

And you, Jinnie—my dramatic man-hater, BTS-obsessed, orange-cat-loving jealous crybaby, possessive, annoying, and impossibly adorable. ✦

Neither of us knew that a stranger on the other side of a screen would become such a familiar part of life.

Day five: a chaotic orbit

Five days of talking, different platforms, and another block after another.

You kept disappearing. I grew frustrated. You thought I was rude; I thought you took me too lightly.

Two stubborn souls, both too caught up in their own worlds to understand the other.

And then, we stopped talking. ❄️

Three days later, I returned

Maybe my ego had won the argument, but it couldn't convince me that I was entirely right.

So I came back first—not to win, not to argue, but simply to say sorry.

And just like that, a little connection I thought had ended found its way back into my orbit.

A month of little things

BTS reels, K-drama recommendations, shared jokes, and little updates about our days.

We started checking in on each other's lives, sharing the ordinary things that somehow became worth sharing.

No grand promises. No extraordinary moments. Just two people slowly becoming a part of each other's everyday universe. ✦

When another storm reached us

We had found our rhythm, but then our besties' relationship began falling apart.

Somehow, their storm reached us too. Our conversations turned into arguments, and words left their marks.

You felt hurt, and this time, you chose to leave.

Once again, two worlds that had begun to understand each other drifted apart.

Three days later, you returned

This time, it wasn't me crossing the distance.

You came back, acknowledged your part in what happened, and gave us another chance.

Maybe that was when we began to understand something important: we didn't always have to be right to find our way back to each other.

And this time, something between us began to change. 💜

When our frequencies matched

We began sharing more than jokes and daily updates.

Our thoughts, our habits, our perspectives—we discovered little similarities in places we hadn't expected.

You were talkative, I was flirty, and our conversations kept finding new ways to continue.

I stopped hiding my interest in you. Somewhere between your words and my teasing, our wavelengths began to feel like one. ✦

Past the edges of your universe

Our conversations stretched past midnight, then 4 a.m., sometimes even 6.

You slowly let me see the parts of your heart you usually kept behind a shield.

Even on days when fever left you feeling awful, we still found moments to talk.

And somewhere along the way, I became someone you could turn to—a little corner of the universe where you could simply be yourself.

A place in your tomorrow

Then you trusted me with something beyond our everyday conversations: your future.

You let me research colleges for you, helping you explore where your next chapter might begin.

It was more than searching through college websites. It was the quiet feeling of being allowed to help with a dream that belonged to you.

For someone who had once been a stranger behind a screen, that trust meant something. 💜

When words found a voice

Then came hostel life, and our little universe found a new way to exist.

Chats became calls. Three or four hours turned into eight or nine.

We no longer needed endless messages when we could simply hear each other speak.

Miles still stood between us, and we still hadn't met face to face. Yet every call made the distance feel a little less infinite.

My favourite part of every day

Six months ago, you were a stranger I happened to meet through our besties.

Now, you're the person whose updates I look forward to, whose voice I can listen to for hours, and whose little world I genuinely want to understand.

We've had our collisions, our silences, and our returns. But every time we found our way back, our universe grew a little bigger.

And somewhere among all these stars, memories, and songs, my heart quietly began choosing you. ✦💜
